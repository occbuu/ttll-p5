# Aggregate input tables

- `table13b_wards_before.csv`: 34 former-ward modelled population totals for 2025 from **GHS-POP R2023A**, clipped to the project's former-ward polygons, plus polygon areas in km². Population is modelled, not a register or census. Source: European Commission Joint Research Centre, Global Human Settlement Layer; reuse requires acknowledgment. See [GHSL data access](https://ghsl.jrc.ec.europa.eu/documents/GHSL_data_access.pdf).
- `osm_ward_year_counts.csv`: counts and lengths by former ward, OSM category and snapshot through **2026-08-18**. Derived from OpenStreetMap historical extracts; © OpenStreetMap contributors. The derived data are shared under the [Open Database License (ODbL)](https://www.openstreetmap.org/copyright/en). Missing category rows represent zero mapped features for a ward; they do not establish absence in the real world.

The tables contain ward-level aggregate values only. They contain no survey responses, participant identifiers, addresses, feature coordinates or individual OSM objects. The source boundary GeoJSON and raw raster/OSM extracts are not bundled. The tables permit exact reproduction of the scenario results and Figures 2–5. Figure 1 needs the former-ward GeoJSON described in the main README.

- `successor_proposal_2025.csv`: population and area of the 12 successor wards from [Ministry of Home Affairs Dispatch 2896/BNV-CQDP, 27 May 2025, annex p. 75](https://static3.luatvietnam.vn/uploaded/vietlawfile/2025/5/s_2896_bnv_cqdp_290525104723.pdf). The official value for Tang Nhon Phu is 16.51 km², correcting the earlier 16.50 transcription in a newspaper report. The legal predecessor composition is corroborated by [HCMC People's Council Resolution 25/NQ-HDND](https://cdn.thuvienphapluat.vn/uploads/khoinghiep/2025/05/12/nghi-quyet-25nq-hdnd-tphcm-2025-tan-thanh-sap-xep-don-vi-hanh-chinh-cap-xa-da-nen.pdf) and final Resolution 1685/NQ-UBTVQH15. Ward-specific **proposed** positions and the reported three-way source decomposition come from [Phap Luat TP.HCM, 17 May 2025](https://plo.vn/phuong-an-nhan-su-o-tp-thu-duc-khi-thanh-lap-12-phuong-moi-post850208.html). These are planning figures, not verified post-reform staffing or service outcomes. The article itself is not redistributed; its citywide category subtotals need verification against a signed personnel proposal.
